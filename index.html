About

Business systems, built end to end

I'm a software developer based in Riyadh with a Bachelor of Information Technology from the University of Khartoum (Second Class Honours, Division I) and 1+ year of professional experience.

At QAST I build and support internal business systems used daily by staff, team leaders, managers and directors: a Payment Tracker, a Smart Vending Tool, the MyCase Knowledge Management System and a PMO portfolio dashboard. I work across React and TypeScript frontends, Node.js and Java Spring Boot APIs and PostgreSQL, with role-based approval workflows, Postman API tests and regression tests before each release.

On the ERP side I develop Odoo modules in Python, XML, QWeb and OWL. My largest build is an 11-module Hospital Information System on Odoo 19 Enterprise with ZATCA e-invoicing and NPHIES insurance (FHIR). I enjoy turning messy business requirements into clear workflows, and I'm comfortable supporting users in production: triage, root cause analysis and documentation.

Native Arabic speaker with professional English. Open to developer, Odoo, DevOps and application support roles in Saudi Arabia.
