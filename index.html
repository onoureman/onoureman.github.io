About

Software, built and supported end to end

I'm a software developer based in Riyadh with a Bachelor of Information Technology from the University of Khartoum (Second Class Honours, Division I) and 2 years of professional experience.

At QAST I build and support internal business systems used daily by staff, team leaders, managers and directors: a Payment Tracker, a Smart Vending Tool, the MyCase Knowledge Management System and a PMO portfolio dashboard. I work across React and TypeScript frontends, Node.js and Java Spring Boot APIs and PostgreSQL, with role-based approval workflows, Postman API tests and regression tests before each release.

I also look after what happens after release: containerised deployments with Docker on Linux, health checks and log analysis, incident triage, root cause analysis and documentation, following ITIL practices. I have trained on monitoring tools such as Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Splunk and the ELK stack, and I build KPI dashboards and reports for management.

On the ERP side I develop modules in Python, XML, QWeb and OWL on Odoo, including a Hospital Information System with ZATCA e-invoicing and NPHIES insurance integration.

Native Arabic speaker with professional English. Open to software developer, full-stack, DevOps, application support and ERP roles in Saudi Arabia.
